Job Costing & Pricing Methodology

Pricing micro-manufacturing services accurately is essential for makers, service bureaus, and small businesses. Underestimating expenses like power consumption, wear, labor, and failure margins will quickly eliminate profitability.

1. Consumable Material Precision

Calculates exact raw stock used relative to full spool or sheet costs, ensuring leftover scrap overhead is integrated into unit production pricing.

2. Machine Maintenance Allowance

Covers hourly wear rates to build a maintenance fund for replacing nozzles, belts, LCD screens, laser tubes, and focus lenses over time.

3. Energy Utilities & Overhead

Factors in active wattage consumption alongside local utility kilowatt-hour rates to accurately capture true running costs.
